---
title: "Roles and access"
description: "The three account types in Kommerce, and exactly what each one can see and do."
---

Kommerce has three account types. Which one you have determines your entire navigation — not just permissions on individual buttons, but which items appear in the sidebar at all.

| Account type | Who it is | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `agency` | The agency owner | Every brand in the agency, plus agency-level configuration |
| `agency_member` | A team member at the agency | Same workspace as the owner, subject to member permissions |
| `brand` | A brand operating its own account | One brand — their own |

## What each account sees

The sidebar is the clearest expression of the difference.

Brand users get the four workspace items as flat top-level rows, because there is no agency/brand mode to nest them under — they are always in their own brand's context.

## Agency-only features

These are hidden from brand accounts entirely:

- **Skills** — the house-procedure library
- **Automations** — scheduled reports and pulses
- **Integrations** — connecting Meta, Slack, Shopify, ClickUp, and the rest
- **Settings** — agency configuration, members, workspace channels
- **Credits** — the agency credit wallet
- **The Brands list** — brand accounts have exactly one brand and never see a picker

<Note>
  Integrations and Settings are agency-level by design. A brand user cannot connect their own Meta account from inside Kommerce — the agency connects it on their behalf. If you are a brand user who needs an integration changed, ask your agency.
</Note>

## Brand context and the agency "view as brand" mode

Agency users move between brands constantly, so most brand-scoped pages carry a `brandId` in the URL. When an agency user opens the chat home with a brand selected, the sidebar switches into the **brand portal** shape — the same layout a brand user sees.

This is deliberate: it lets an agency operator see the workspace the way their client sees it. The agency footer (Skills, Automations, Integrations, Settings) is hidden in that mode.

Kommerce also remembers the last brand workspace you were in and offers a shortcut back to it from the sidebar.

<Warning>
  A brand user's context is pinned to their own brand and cannot be changed. If a brand-scoped page ever appears empty, it usually means the brand has not been fully onboarded yet — not that you lack permission.
</Warning>
