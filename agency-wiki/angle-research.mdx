Angle research is the work you do before creative. Kommerce runs it as a **brand audit** you can then interrogate in chat, and the output feeds every downstream brief.

Open **Media Buying → Marketing Report** for the brand, or go to `/marketing-report` with the brand selected.

## Run the audit

The audit reads the brand's website, products, and personas, and produces a positioning analysis titled **Brand Audit — `<brand name>`**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the brand is onboarded">
    The audit reads from the brand guide. A brand still in **Discovered** or **Processing** will produce thin results. Check the Brands list first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the marketing report">
    Kommerce assembles the audit from the brand's products and site. This runs against live data, so give it a moment on first load.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the audit, then interrogate it">
    The report is a starting point, not the deliverable. The value is in the follow-up questions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The four starter questions

The report ships with four prompts because they are the four questions that actually change what you build next:

| Prompt | What it gets you |
| --- | --- |
| **Top takeaways** | The three most important findings and why they matter |
| **Biggest gap** | Where your positioning diverges from competitors — the whitespace |
| **Best persona** | Which persona to prioritise next, and the angle to lead with |
| **Quick wins** | Three actions you can take this week |

**Best persona** is the one that matters most for the next step. Its answer gives you a persona *and* an angle, which is exactly the pair the creative brief asks for.

<Tip>
  Treat the audit as a conversation, not a document. Ask it to compare two personas, to argue against its own recommendation, or to explain why a competitor's angle works. The follow-ups are where the useful positioning comes from.
</Tip>

## What feeds the audit

Angle quality is bounded by brand context quality. Four things feed it:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Brand guide" icon="book">
    Voice, ICP, competitors, and communication rules. Editable at **Brand Guide**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Products" icon="box">
    Names, descriptions, imagery, and URLs captured during onboarding.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Personas" icon="users">
    The audiences you sell to. Selected directly in the creative brief.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Website" icon="globe">
    Prefilled by scraping the brand's site during onboarding.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

If the audit reads generically, the fix is almost always upstream: fill in the brand guide rather than re-running the audit.

## From angle to creative

The audit gives you a positioning direction. The **creative brief** turns it into an executable angle with hooks and a CTA.

You do not copy text between them by hand — the brief reads the same brand context. What you carry across is the *decision*: which product, which persona, which awareness level.

## Start a campaign from the report

The report offers a **Start a Campaign** action that carries the brand context straight into the campaign wizard. Use it when the audit has already told you what to build and you do not need a separate creative pass.
