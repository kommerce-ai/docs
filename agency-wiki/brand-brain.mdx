## The problem it solves

Kommerce already produces substantial brand knowledge and then loses most of it. Meta audits, uploaded brand books and guideline PDFs, personas, creative guardrails, competitor research — all of it exists, and almost none of it reaches the agent when you ask a question.

Uploaded documents are the clearest case: a brand book gets compressed once into a fixed set of fields during onboarding, and the original is never read again. Everything in it that did not fit those fields is gone.

Brand Brain is the fix — a per-brand corpus the agent can actually query, with citations, instead of a fixed bundle pushed into every conversation.

## The intended design

A per-brand knowledge base held as documents in a shared Google Drive folder, projected into Kommerce, maintained as an interlinked wiki, and retrieved by [Kenny](/agency-wiki/kenny) through a budgeted surface.

Two things about the design are worth knowing because they explain its shape:

**Drive is the shared workspace.** Agency and client work in the same folder. Kommerce publishes its own generated artefacts — audits, reports, briefs — back into it, so the corpus grows from both sides.

**Slack and ClickUp are deliberately excluded** as document sources. One active Slack channel would consume between one and five times a brand's entire corpus budget on its own. That activity reaches the brain through the [daily and weekly pulse](/agency-wiki/reporting) instead, already summarised.

| Component | Status |

| Chunking and embeddings | Not shipped |
| Entity graph | Not shipped |
| The wiki layer | Not shipped |
| Retrieval with citations | Not shipped |
| **AI Brain** tab in the UI | **Disabled** |

The document store is the foundation phase. It gives every unit of brand knowledge a stable identity so later phases have something to build on — but on its own it changes nothing you can see.

<Note>
  Documents are **tombstoned, never deleted**. Once the wiki layer exists, pages will cite documents by ID, and a hard delete would leave citations pointing at nothing with no way to render "source removed".
</Note>

## Why the AI Brain tab is off

The tab is hidden in the navigation and force-disabled on the report surface. A stale `?tab=brain` URL is redirected to Overview rather than loading.

The reason is its graph backend, which was returning errors. The tab is commented out rather than removed, so restoring it is a small change once that backend is available — but until then it is genuinely off, not merely hidden.

<Warning>
  If a client asks about "the AI Brain" because they saw it mentioned, tell them it is not yet available. Do not point them at a `?tab=brain` URL — it will not work.
</Warning>

## What to do in the meantime

The corpus will be built from material that already exists, so quality now compounds later:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Keep brand guides genuinely complete">
    The brand guide is the highest-value document in any brand's corpus. See [Angle research](/agency-wiki/angle-research) for why thin context degrades everything downstream.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the real source documents">
    Brand books, guideline PDFs, and strategy docs. Today only a compressed subset is used, but the originals are what the corpus will eventually draw on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep Drive organised per brand">
    The shared Drive folder is the intended ingest surface, so structure you impose now is structure the brain inherits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What works today instead

For grounded answers about a brand right now, use [Kenny](/agency-wiki/kenny). He reads brand context, personas, products, and the brand guide, and takes a live snapshot of the page you are on. That is the working retrieval path — narrower than Brand Brain intends to be, but real.
