Kommerce meters the work that costs money to run — copy generation, image and video generation, and agent runs — and debits it from a credit balance.

Open the wallet at `/agency/credits`.

## The wallet

The page shows your **agency balance** and lets you **add credits**, either from preset amounts or a **custom amount**.

The billable unit is the **brand**. Usage is attributed to whichever brand the work was done for, so you can see where generation spend is going across the portfolio.

## What consumes credits

| Operation | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Creative generation** | The largest consumer. Cost scales with the size of your generation matrix. |
| **Video generation** | Heavier per asset than images. |
| **Agent runs** | Chat turns, audits, and skill runs. |
| **Scheduled automations** | Each pulse composes a message, which is an agent run. |

<Warning>
  The creative studio multiplies: `angles × products × awareness levels × personas × variations`. Selecting every awareness level and a high variation count turns a single brief into a very large job. Check the count on the Templates step before you hit Create.
</Warning>

## Keeping balances healthy

Balances are decremented as work completes and low-balance signals fire as you approach zero.

Top-ups are **manual** — there is no auto-recharge. For brands running continuous generation, check the balance as part of your weekly rhythm rather than waiting for a failure.

<Note>
  Accounting is backed by an append-only ledger, and the displayed balance is derived from it. If a balance ever looks wrong, the ledger is the source of truth and the balance can be rebuilt from it — raise it rather than working around it.
</Note>

## Reducing spend

The most effective lever is the brief, not the settings:

- **Narrow the awareness levels.** Generating for all levels when you only intend to run one is the most common source of waste.
- **Review the brief before generating.** A reviewed angle produces usable assets on the first pass; an unreviewed one produces a large batch you discard.
- **Lower the variation count** while you are still exploring, and raise it once an angle is proven.
