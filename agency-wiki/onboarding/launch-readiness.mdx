Launch readiness is a separate ledger from step completion. Steps *produce* readiness items; your agency *verifies* them. A brand is launch-ready only when every required item is verified.

## The checklist

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Required — blocks launch" icon="lock">
    - Logo
    - Product photos
    - Website access
    - Meta partner access
    - Business verification
    - Meta ad account
    - Payment method
  </Card>
  <Card title="Optional — never blocks" icon="lock-open">
    - Product video
    - Instagram
    - Meta Pixel
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  **Every item is owned by the brand**, including business verification and payment method. There is nothing on this list your agency can supply on their behalf — which is why chasing it is the work, and why it is tracked explicitly rather than assumed.
</Note>

Meta Pixel being optional is worth a second look. It will not block launch, but a missing or misfiring pixel is the most common Red on the [Meta audit's](/agency-wiki/meta-audit) Signal dimension — and Signal Red makes every downstream number unreliable.

## Item status

```
missing → received → verified
```

- **Missing** — not provided yet
- **Received** — a step produced it; not yet confirmed
- **Verified** — your agency confirmed it, or the server did

Brand artifacts leave `missing` when their producing step completes. Meta items leave `missing` only once verification succeeds.

## Still needed

The **still needed** list is exactly the required items still sitting at `missing`. It is the chase list — work it directly rather than reading step statuses and inferring.

<Tip>
  Check still-needed before the kickoff call, not after. Business verification and payment method are the two items with real external latency — Meta's own verification can take days, and it is the most common reason a brand that "finished onboarding" cannot actually spend.
</Tip>

## Intake complete is not launch ready

A brand showing **Fully onboarded** in the Brands list has finished all three steps. That is intake, not the go-live gate.

The gap between them is almost always business verification or payment method — both required, both owned by the brand, and neither produced by simply completing a form in Kommerce.

<Warning>
  Do not schedule a launch date off "Fully onboarded". Check launch readiness, and specifically whether business verification is verified rather than received.
</Warning>

## Before you spend

Once launch-ready, run a [Meta audit](/agency-wiki/meta-audit) before the first campaign — especially on an account with existing history. Its most common finding is broken tracking, and every optimisation made on top of that is wasted work.
