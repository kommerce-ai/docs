<Warning>
  **Not available yet.** Brand Brain is still being built. There's nothing for you to open or configure today — this page explains what it is and what you can do now so it's useful when it arrives.
</Warning>

## What it's for

Right now, a lot of what you tell Kommerce about your brand gets compressed into a fixed set of fields and the original is never read again. If you uploaded a brand book during onboarding, everything in it that didn't fit those fields is effectively gone.

Brand Brain fixes that. The idea is a proper knowledge base for your brand — your documents, your reports, your research — that [Kenny](/brands-wiki/kenny) can actually search and quote from, instead of working off a fixed summary.

## What it will mean for you

- Ask questions about your own material and get answers that cite the source
- Uploaded documents stay useful instead of being read once and discarded
- Reports and research Kommerce produces for you become part of the same searchable body of knowledge

## What's live today

The groundwork — a store that gives every document a stable identity. It runs in the background and there's no screen for it.

The parts you'd actually notice — search, citations, the **AI Brain** view — haven't shipped. If you've seen the AI Brain tab mentioned anywhere, it's currently switched off.

## What you can do now

Everything the brain will eventually draw on is material you're already providing, so effort now isn't wasted:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in your brand guide properly">
    It'll be the single most valuable document in your brand's knowledge base — and it already improves everything Kommerce writes for you today. See [Your brand guide](/brands-wiki/brand-guide).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your real documents to your agency">
    Brand books, guidelines, strategy decks. Today only a summary gets used, but the originals are what the brain will read later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep product information current">
    Products and personas feed both today's briefs and tomorrow's knowledge base.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to use instead for now

[Kenny](/brands-wiki/kenny) already reads your brand context, products, personas, and brand guide — and he can see the page you're on when you ask. It's narrower than what Brand Brain intends to be, but it works today.

<Note>
  Your agency manages what goes into your brand's document store. If there's material you want Kommerce to know about, send it to them rather than looking for an upload screen.
</Note>
