## Step 1 — Brief

This is the step that decides quality. Everything after it is execution.

| Field | What to put in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Product** | Which product this ad sells. Required. |
| **Persona** | Who it talks to. |
| **Awareness** | How much your reader already knows about you. |
| **Theme** | Optional — a season, a campaign, a claim you want led with. |
| **Variations** | How many versions per awareness level. |

Click generate and you'll get an **angle, hooks, and a CTA**. These are editable, and editing them is the point of this step.

Read them and ask whether you'd actually run them. If not, fix them here — a bad angle across twenty templates is twenty bad ads.

Then click **Continue to templates**.

### Awareness, and why it matters most

This is the field people skip. It changes the first line of every ad:

- Someone who doesn't know they have the problem needs the problem named first
- Someone who knows the problem but not your solution needs the mechanism
- Someone who already knows you needs a reason to act now

One ad written for all three lands with none of them.

## Step 2 — Templates

Pick from the **Select Visual Blueprints** grid, grouped by product category. Templates are the visual shell; your brief's angles fill them.

<Warning>
  Work out what you're about to generate before clicking Create. The count is roughly `angles × products × awareness levels × personas × variations` — three angles, three awareness levels, two personas and three variations is **54 assets from one click**. It draws on your operator's credit balance.
</Warning>

Start with a few templates. You can always run the same brief against more later.

## Step 3 — Generate

Assets stream in as they finish, with placeholders for what's still rendering. You don't need to sit and watch — they'll be in your library.

## Getting better results

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start narrow">
    One product, one persona, one awareness level, three variations. See if the angle lands before scaling it up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Theme">
    It's the easiest way to steer a whole batch without rewriting the brief.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the hooks directly">
    The hooks are your copy. Changing them is faster and more predictable than regenerating and hoping.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Things to know

- **Regenerating overwrites the asset.** No undo — download first if you might want it back.
- **Your product photos set the ceiling.** Templates composite them, so a low-resolution or text-covered source photo carries that into every ad.
