Video has five steps. It starts like the image studio — with a brief that produces an angle, hooks, and a CTA — then adds what a video needs.

Open **Creative Production** and switch the toggle to **Video**.

## The five steps

| Step | What it decides |
| --- | --- |
| **Brief** | Your message — angle, hooks, CTA |
| **Style** | How it looks |
| **Cast** | Who's in it |
| **Script** | What gets said, built from your hooks |
| **Post-production** | Final assembly |

<Note>
  Your progress is saved. Video takes longer than images, so you can leave partway through and come back to the step you were on.
</Note>

## Step 1 — Brief

First you set context — product, persona, and format. Then you choose how the message gets written:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Generate new" icon="sparkles">
    Written from your product and brand context. The usual choice.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Upload Brief" icon="file-arrow-up">
    Drop a `.docx`, `.pdf`, `.md`, or `.txt` if you already have a brief written.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reference Ad" icon="link">
    Drop an ad you like — a video file or a link — and work from that.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

All three end up in the same place: an editable **angle, hooks, and CTA**.

<Tip>
  **Reference Ad** is worth knowing about. If there's an ad you keep pointing at and saying "something like this", dropping it in works far better than trying to describe it in words.
</Tip>

Review the hooks carefully here — the script step builds directly on them, so a weak hook carries all the way through.

## Steps 2 and 3 — Style and Cast

**Style** is how the video looks. **Cast** is who appears in it.

They're separate on purpose: you can change the look without recasting, or recast without redoing the look.

## Step 4 — Script

Built from your hooks. If a line isn't working, it's usually better to fix the **hook** and let the script follow than to rewrite the script by itself.

## Step 5 — Post-production

Final assembly, and where you review the finished video.

## Video or image?

| | Image | Video |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steps | 3 | 5 |
| Speed | Fast | Much slower |
| Cost | Lower | Higher per asset |
| Best for | Testing lots of angles | An angle you've already proven |

<Warning>
  Don't use video to find out whether an angle works. Test cheaply with static images first, then make video of the one that won. Testing at video cost burns through credits fast with little to show for it.
</Warning>
