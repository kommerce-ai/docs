There are two studios. Both start with a **brief** — you decide the message before anything gets made.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Image generation" icon="image" href="/brands-wiki/creative/image-generation">
    Brief → Templates → Generate. Static ads, quickly and in volume.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Video generation" icon="film" href="/brands-wiki/creative/video-generation">
    Brief → Style → Cast → Script → Post-production.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Open **Creative Production** in your sidebar. The Image/Video toggle is at the top.

## What they have in common

Both read the same products and personas, so anything you set up in one is available in the other.

Both briefs produce the same three things: an **angle**, some **hooks**, and a **CTA**. That is your message. Whether it becomes a static image or a video is a separate decision.

<Note>
  The brief is the step worth slowing down on. The angle decides whether an ad works — the template or the cast is just how it gets made.
</Note>

## You can't skip steps

The studios won't let you jump ahead. No templates before a brief, no rendering before templates.

That's deliberate. Generating a big batch from a brief you haven't read produces a lot of ads you then throw away — and generation costs money from your agency's balance.

## Where your ads go

Finished assets land in your creative library, reachable from **Creative Production**. From there they're available when you or your agency [build a campaign](/brands-wiki/media-buying).

## Reviewing what came out

Open an individual asset to look at it properly and regenerate if it missed.

<Warning>
  Regenerating replaces the asset — there's no version history and no undo. If there's one you might want back, download it first.
</Warning>

## If the output feels generic

It's almost never the studio. Check these in order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your brand guide">
    A vague voice description produces vague copy no matter how good the brief is. See [Your brand guide](/brands-wiki/brand-guide).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your product">
    A one-line description and no photos leaves generation with nothing to work from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How broad the brief was">
    One product, one persona, one awareness level, with a specific theme. Broad in, broad out.
  </Step>
</Steps>
