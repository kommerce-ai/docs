You have two kinds of reporting: the performance you look at, and the update that arrives on a schedule.

## Performance in the app

Your brand home shows performance for the linked Meta ad account, including revenue and active campaigns.

You can change the **reporting window** — "last N days" — to widen or narrow what you are looking at. If the numbers look wrong, check the window before anything else.

<Note>
  If the home surface shows no account linked, your Meta ad account has not been connected yet. See [Media buying](/brands-wiki/media-buying#linking-your-meta-ad-account).
</Note>

## The marketing report

The marketing report is an interactive brand audit rather than a metrics dashboard — positioning, competitor gaps, and which persona to lead with.

It ships with four starter questions:

| Question | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Top takeaways** | The three findings that matter most, and why |
| **Biggest gap** | Where your positioning differs from competitors |
| **Best persona** | Which audience to prioritise, and the angle to lead with |
| **Quick wins** | Three things you could do this week |

It is a conversation. Follow up — ask it to compare two personas, or to argue against its own recommendation.

**Best persona** is the most directly useful, because its answer gives you the persona *and* the angle — the exact pair a [creative brief](/brands-wiki/creative/overview) asks for.

## Scheduled updates

Your operator can set up a recurring pulse that posts to Slack. The daily version has a fixed three-section shape:

1. **What we're working on** — current work in progress
2. **Results** — performance over the window
3. **Agenda** — what needs attention

Sections are assembled from your linked integrations. Each is independent — if one source is unavailable, that section notes it and the rest still posts.

<Note>
  Automations are configured by your operator, not by you. If an update stops arriving, arrives at the wrong hour, or has a section that is always empty, tell your operator — the usual causes are timezone configuration or the Slack bot not being in the channel it needs to read.
</Note>

## Attribution

Performance figures come from Meta's own attribution.

If you have Shopify connected and Meta-reported purchases diverge noticeably from what your store shows, that gap is worth raising with your operator. A divergence beyond roughly 25% usually means a tracking problem rather than a reporting one, and it makes every optimisation decision on top of it unreliable.

<Tip>
  Before concluding that performance dropped, check whether tracking changed. A pixel that stopped firing looks exactly like a campaign that stopped working.
</Tip>
