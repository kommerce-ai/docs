Your workspace covers one brand — yours. There is no brand picker and no account switching, because everything you see is already scoped to you.

## Your sidebar

| Item | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Chat. Describe what you want and Kommerce routes it. |
| **My Brand** | Your brand's context — guide, products, personas. |
| **Media Buying** | Campaigns and performance. |
| **Creative Production** | Briefs, generation, and your asset library. |

That is the whole app. It is deliberately narrow.

## What your agency handles

These exist in Kommerce but are agency-level, so they are not in your sidebar:

- **Integrations** — connecting Meta, Shopify, Slack, and ClickUp
- **Settings** — workspace configuration
- **Skills and Automations** — the procedures and scheduled reports your agency runs
- **Credits** — the balance that funds generation

<Note>
  If you need a Meta account relinked, a Slack channel changed, or an integration connected, ask your agency. You cannot make those changes yourself, and that is by design rather than a permissions gap.
</Note>

## The one thing you do in Meta directly

Granting **partner access** to your agency's Business Manager happens in Meta, not in Kommerce.

You stay the owner of your ad account. Your agency gets management partner access, scoped to the ad account, page, and pixel. Nothing spends or posts without your approval, and you can revoke access at any time.

You enter your own ad account ID in Kommerce and click **Verify** — Kommerce checks server-side that access came through.

<Warning>
  Only ever enter your **own** ad account ID. If verification says the account is already linked to another brand, stop and tell your agency rather than trying other IDs.
</Warning>

## Getting your workspace useful

Output quality depends almost entirely on how complete your brand context is.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Finish onboarding">
    Brand details, products and assets, and Meta access. Some of it may be prefilled from your website for you to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the brand guide">
    Voice, communication rules, and products. This is what every brief and report reads from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your products and personas">
    Creative briefs ask you to pick one of each. If they are thin or missing, briefs will be generic.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If creative or reports come back sounding like they could be about any brand, the fix is nearly always the brand guide — not the brief you just wrote.
</Tip>
