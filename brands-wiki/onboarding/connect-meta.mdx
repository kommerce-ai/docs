This is the only onboarding step that happens outside Kommerce. You grant your operator partner access inside your own Meta Business Manager, then confirm it back in Kommerce.

<Info>
  **You stay the owner of your ad account.** Your operator gets management partner access, scoped to the ad account, page, and pixel. Nothing spends without your approval, and you can revoke access at any time from your Business Manager.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agency ID">
    Kommerce shows your operator's Business Manager ID with a copy button. Copy it — you'll paste it into Meta.

    <Note>
      The button is labelled **Copy agency ID** in the product. It means the same thing as your operator's Business Manager ID.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Meta Business Manager">
    There's a direct link on the same screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant partner access">
    Add the operator as a partner using the ID you copied, and assign them your ad account, page, and pixel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your ad account ID">
    Back in Kommerce, enter your own ad account ID. It looks like `act_1234567890` and is found in your Business Manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Verify">
    Kommerce checks that access came through.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If verification fails

You'll see **We couldn't find that ad account yet. Try again.** Work through these in order — the first is by far the most common:

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Right agency ID?** | Re-compare what you pasted in Meta against the ID Kommerce shows. Pasting the wrong business is the usual cause. |
| **Just granted it?** | Access can take a moment to propagate. Wait and retry. |
| **Right ID type?** | It must be the **ad account** ID with the `act_` prefix — not a page ID or business ID. |
| **Assets assigned?** | Adding the partner isn't enough on its own. The ad account has to be assigned to them. |

Retrying is safe and unlimited — nothing is consumed by a failed check.

<Warning>
  Only ever enter **your own** ad account ID. If Kommerce says the account is already linked to another brand, stop and tell your operator. That guard exists to stop one brand accidentally claiming another's account, and working around it causes a real problem rather than solving one.
</Warning>

## Why you enter the ID rather than picking from a list

A list would be simpler — and it would mean showing you every ad account your operator can access, including their other clients'.

Instead you submit only your own ID, and the check runs on the server against your operator's credentials. Their client list is never exposed to you.

## What this unlocks

Completing this step moves four items on your [launch checklist](/brands-wiki/onboarding/launch-checklist) toward verified: Meta partner access, your ad account, business verification, and payment method.

It moves the needle more than any other single action — which is also why it's the step most worth doing early.

<Note>
  Verification here confirms **ad account** access. Publishing an ad also needs permission on your Facebook **Page**, which is granted separately. If ads later fail to publish while campaigns are created fine, mention it to your operator — that's the usual cause and it's fixable from Meta.
</Note>
