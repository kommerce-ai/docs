This step gives Kommerce the things it builds ads out of. Creative generation composes your product imagery into templates, so the quality ceiling of every ad you generate is set here.

## What it covers

- **Products** — names, descriptions, imagery
- **Logo** — required before launch
- **Product photos** — required before launch
- **Product video** — optional
- **Website** — your site

## Products

Each product needs a name, a description, and imagery.

<Warning>
  A product with a one-line description and no photos will produce weak creative no matter how good your brief is. Generation has nothing to work with. If you only have time for one thing in this step, put it into your best-selling product rather than spreading thin coverage across the catalogue.
</Warning>

Write descriptions the way you'd explain the product to a customer, not the way a spec sheet would. The description feeds copy, so ingredient lists and dimensions are less useful than what the product does and who it's for.

## Photography

Photos are used directly in generated ads, so resolution and framing carry through.

What works best:

- **Clean product shots on plain backgrounds** — these composite into templates most reliably
- **Multiple angles** — gives generation options
- **Lifestyle shots** — useful, but keep some isolated product shots too

What causes trouble:

- Images with text baked in — the template adds its own copy on top
- Heavy shadows or busy backgrounds — these fight the template
- Low resolution — it will not improve on the way through

## Logo

Required before launch. Supply the highest resolution you have, ideally with a transparent background.

## Website

Your site URL. It is what Kommerce scraped for any prefilled content, and it stays linked as context.

**Website access** is a separate required item on the launch checklist — that means genuine access, not just the URL.

## If it was drafted for you

A scrape will usually find your products and pull their images automatically. Check:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Did every product come through?">
    Products behind a filter, in a dropdown, or out of stock at scrape time are often missed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Are the images the right ones?">
    Scraping grabs what it finds, which may be a thumbnail or a lifestyle crop rather than your best product shot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the descriptions read like marketing or like a spec?">
    Site copy is often spec-shaped. Rewrite the ones you plan to advertise.
  </Step>
</Steps>
