Onboarding is where Kommerce learns your brand. Everything the product produces afterwards — ad copy, creative, positioning research, reports — reads from what you provide here.

It is worth doing properly once. Thin input here shows up as generic output everywhere else, and re-running a brief will not fix it.

## What you'll be asked for

| Step | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand details** | Who you are, how you sound, who you sell to, who you compete with |
| **Products & assets** | Your products, logo, photos, and website |
| **Connect Meta ad account** | Giving your agency access to run ads for you |

You may not see all three. If your agency is handling some of them for you, you will only see the steps assigned to you.

## Some of it may be filled in already

Kommerce can prefill **Brand details** and **Products & assets** by reading your website. When it has, the step is marked **We drafted this — review**.

<Warning>
  Treat a draft as a starting point, not a finished answer. Scraping gets the shape right and the specifics wrong — it will usually capture roughly what you sell and miss how you actually talk about it. The parts it gets wrong are exactly the parts that matter most downstream.
</Warning>

**Connect Meta ad account** is never prefilled. It needs you to act inside your own Meta Business Manager.

## What the labels mean

| Label | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **This step needs you** | It's yours, and it isn't finished |
| **We drafted this — review** | We filled it in; check and correct it |
| **Waiting on agency** | Your agency has this one |
| **Done** | Complete |

## Two finish lines

Finishing the steps and being ready to run ads are different things, and the gap between them catches people out.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Steps complete" icon="list-check">
    You've filled everything in. Kommerce now has what it needs to produce good work.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Launch ready" icon="rocket">
    Everything needed to actually spend is verified — including things Meta has to confirm, like business verification.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Your progress bar tracks the **steps**. The [launch checklist](/brands-wiki/onboarding/launch-checklist) tracks the second one, and it is the one that decides when ads can go live.

<Tip>
  Start business verification in Meta early, even before you finish the other steps. It is the item with the longest external wait — Meta can take days — and it blocks launch regardless of how quickly you complete everything else.
</Tip>
