Finishing the onboarding steps and being ready to run ads are two different milestones. The checklist below is the second one, and it is what actually gates spend.

## What's required

Every one of these has to be verified before ads can run:

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Logo** | High resolution, transparent background if you have it |
| **Product photos** | At least for the products you plan to advertise |
| **Website access** | Genuine access, not just the URL |
| **Meta partner access** | Granted in your Business Manager |
| **Business verification** | Verified by Meta — start this early |
| **Meta ad account** | Linked and verified |
| **Payment method** | On your Meta ad account |

## Optional

These never block launch:

- **Product video**
- **Instagram**
- **Meta Pixel**

<Warning>
  The Pixel is technically optional, but treat it as required in practice. Without it firing correctly, campaign performance can't be measured properly — and every optimisation decision made on top of bad tracking is unreliable. A pixel that stopped firing looks exactly like a campaign that stopped working.
</Warning>

## Item status

Each item moves through three states:

- **Missing** — not provided yet
- **Received** — you've supplied it; not yet confirmed
- **Verified** — confirmed

Only **verified** counts toward launch. An item sitting at received still blocks you.

## Everything here is yours

All of these items are owned by you, not your operator — including business verification and payment method. There is nothing on this list your operator can supply on your behalf.

That is not an oversight. Each one either lives in your Meta account or is something only you can provide, which is why they're tracked individually rather than assumed.

## What usually holds things up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Business verification">
    The single most common blocker. Meta's own verification process can take days and is entirely outside Kommerce. **Start it before you do anything else** — you can complete every other item while it's pending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment method">
    Easy to forget because it feels like a billing detail rather than a setup step. No payment method means no spend, regardless of everything else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Product photos">
    Often the last thing gathered. Worth doing early since creative quality depends on it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## "Steps complete" is not "ready to launch"

If your onboarding shows as finished but your operator says you're not ready to run ads, the gap is almost always business verification or payment method — both required, both yours, and neither completed by filling in a form in Kommerce.

<Tip>
  Ask your operator for your still-needed list rather than inferring from step progress. It shows exactly which required items are still missing, which is the actual work remaining.
</Tip>
