Kenny is the assistant behind the chat in your workspace. He isn't a help bot — he can read your brand context, make creative, build campaigns, and pull your live performance numbers.

Your Home screen is chat-first: you describe what you want instead of hunting for the right page.

## What he can do

- Read and analyse your brand context — products, personas, brand guide
- Create and edit campaigns, ad sets, and creative
- Generate and refine ad copy and headlines
- Research competitors
- Pull live performance data from your connected accounts
- Work with your connected Shopify store

## Where to find him

Kenny opens either as a **side panel** next to what you're doing, or as a **full-screen chat** for longer sessions.

## He can see the page you're on

Worth knowing, because it changes how you should ask.

Kenny takes a snapshot of your current screen **at the moment you hit send**. So asking "why did this drop?" while looking at a campaign works — he's reading that campaign's real numbers, not guessing from what you typed.

Two things follow from that:

- **Ask from the page you're asking about.** You'll get a better answer about a campaign from the campaign screen than from your settings.
- **The suggested prompts change per page.** When the chat is empty, what's offered is what makes sense where you are.

## Quick actions

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Create image** | Starts making creative |
| **Marketing summary** | Pulls your current performance together |
| **Brand analysis** | Reviews your positioning and brand context |
| **Copywriting ideas** | Generates copy and headlines |
| **Competitive audit** | Researches your competitors |

## Why some answers take longer

Some requests are a single lookup — "what did I spend yesterday?" comes back fast.

Others hand off to a specialist that does real multi-step work. "Build me a campaign for the new product" is doing genuine orchestration behind the scenes, and it takes correspondingly longer.

A pause on the second kind means it's working, not stuck.

## He'll offer to remember things

When Kenny learns something new about your brand — a customer type, a preference, something you always want avoided — he'll say so and offer to save it.

<Tip>
  Say yes when he's right. Everything Kommerce writes for you reads from your brand context, so a fact saved once improves every ad and report afterwards. Declining means telling him again next time.
</Tip>

## Good things to ask

- *"Why did my cost per purchase go up this week?"*
- *"Write me three hooks for [product] aimed at first-time buyers"*
- *"What's my best performing creative this month, and why do you think it's working?"*
- *"What are my competitors leading with right now?"*
- *"Summarise how last week went"*

<Note>
  Kenny is grounded in your brand context, so vague questions get vague answers. "Write me some ads" is weaker than "write hooks for [product] for someone who doesn't know they have this problem yet" — the second tells him the product and the awareness level.
</Note>

## What he can't do

- **Change your integrations or account settings** — those are handled by your agency
- **Spend money.** Anything launched to Meta arrives **paused**. Nothing runs until someone activates it in Ads Manager.
