Open **Brands** at `/operator/brands` and click **Add a brand**.

## Create the record

Enter the brand name and email, optionally with a website. Supplying the website is worth it — it is what the AI prefill scrapes for the first two steps.

Two ways to create:

- **Add brand only** — creates the record without starting onboarding. The brand shows as **Discovered**.
- **Start Onboarding** — creates the record and begins the process.

Creating a brand under your portfolio also provisions its workspace: a member-only Slack channel, and the ClickUp space and Drive folder used later by [automations](/operator-wiki/automations).

## Choose the preset

This sets the default assignee for all three steps. You are choosing a starting point, not a permanent arrangement.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Let the brand do it" icon="envelope">
    Every step assigned to the brand. An invite goes out and you watch progress through live review.
  </Card>
  <Card title="You do it for the brand" icon="user-gear">
    Every step assigned to you. You can still send the invite and hand back individual steps.
  </Card>

<Tip>
  The most reliable split in practice is **you take Brand details and Products & assets, the brand takes Connect Meta**. The first two are work you can do faster from the website scrape; the third is the only one the brand genuinely has to do themselves, because it happens inside their own Business Manager.
</Tip>

## The invite

The brand receives an invite linking to a signup URL carrying a one-time code. Signing up through that link binds their account to you.

A brand that signs up **without** the code lands as a self-serve brand under the house operator, not under you. If a client tells you they signed up but you cannot see them in your Brands list, this is almost always why.

<Warning>
  Send the invite from Kommerce rather than telling a client to "just sign up". The code is what creates the link between their account and you, and there is no clean way to re-parent an account that signed up standalone.
</Warning>

## What the brand sees

A brand only sees the steps assigned to it. If you kept **Brand details** and **Products & assets**, their onboarding shows one step, not three — which is the point of the split.

## Watching progress

Live review shows each step's status and who it is waiting on, so you can see a stall without asking. The usual stall is [Connect Meta](/operator-wiki/onboarding/meta-access) — it is the one step you cannot do for them and the one with an external dependency.

## Brand status in the list

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Discovered** | Added, onboarding not started |
| **Processing** | Onboarding underway |
| **Completed** / **Fully onboarded** | All three steps done |

<Note>
  "Fully onboarded" means intake is complete — not that the brand is safe to spend. That is a separate gate. See [Launch readiness](/operator-wiki/onboarding/launch-readiness).
</Note>
