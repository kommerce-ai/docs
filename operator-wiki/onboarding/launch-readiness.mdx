The readiness ledger tracks what a brand still owes. Steps *produce* readiness items; you *verify* them.

<Warning>
  **The ledger does not gate the `launch_ready` flag.** That flag is purely step-based — intake plus Connect Meta. Required items still missing show up as the **still needed** list, and a brand can read as launch-ready with items outstanding.

  They still matter: Meta enforces most of them itself. A brand without a payment method or business verification cannot spend, whatever Kommerce says.
</Warning>

## The checklist

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Required — on the still-needed list" icon="lock">
    - Logo
    - Product photos
    - Website access
    - Meta partner access
    - Business verification
    - Meta ad account
    - Payment method
  </Card>
  <Card title="Optional — never blocks" icon="lock-open">
    - Product video
    - Instagram
    - Meta Pixel
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  **Every item is owned by the brand**, including business verification and payment method. There is nothing on this list you can supply on their behalf — which is why chasing it is the work, and why it is tracked explicitly rather than assumed.
</Note>

Meta Pixel being optional is worth a second look. It will not block launch, but a missing or misfiring pixel is the most common Red on the [Meta audit's](/operator-wiki/audit/meta-audit) Signal dimension — and Signal Red makes every downstream number unreliable.

## Item status

```
missing → received → verified
```

- **Missing** — not provided yet
- **Received** — a step produced it; not yet confirmed
- **Verified** — you confirmed it, or the server did

Brand artifacts leave `missing` when their producing step completes. Meta items leave `missing` only once verification succeeds.

## Still needed

The **still needed** list is exactly the required items still sitting at `missing`. It is the chase list — work it directly rather than reading step statuses and inferring.

<Tip>
  Check still-needed before the kickoff call, not after. Business verification and payment method are the two items with real external latency — Meta's own verification can take days, and it is the most common reason a brand that reads as launch-ready cannot actually spend.
</Tip>

## Two gates, and neither is the whole story

**Launch ready** is Kommerce's gate — intake plus Connect Meta. It unlocks **Start kickoff**.

**The still-needed list** is Meta's gate in practice. Nothing in Kommerce blocks on it, but Meta will: no payment method means no spend, and unverified business means no spend, regardless of what the brand's status says.

<Warning>
  Do not schedule a launch date off the launch-ready flag alone. It can be true while business verification is still pending, because that item does not feed the flag. Check the still-needed list separately.
</Warning>

## Before you spend

Once launch-ready, run a [Meta audit](/operator-wiki/audit/meta-audit) before the first campaign — especially on an account with existing history. Its most common finding is broken tracking, and every optimisation made on top of that is wasted work.
