## Generation is quoted before it spends

Asking to generate without confirming returns a **price**, not assets. Nothing is spent at that point.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request the generation">
    You get back a quote for what it would cost.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show the quote to whoever owns the budget">
    Especially on a large matrix — the count climbs faster than people expect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-request with confirmation">
    Only now does it spend.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The wallet is a hard ceiling. A run that would exceed the balance is **refused outright** — it is never partially completed, so you will not find half a matrix rendered and the rest missing.
</Warning>

Renders take roughly two minutes. Check back rather than waiting on them.

## Generating for a test specifically

Ideas in a test that have briefs but no ads yet can be generated together, so the whole test is produced as a set rather than one at a time. Same quote-then-confirm rule.

## Reviewing what came back

Zoom in before judging an asset. Text that looks wrong at thumbnail size is often fine close up, and the reverse happens just as often.

Then close the loop:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Score** | Record how good it is |
| **Comment** | Mark the *region* at fault, not just the asset |
| **Regenerate** | Fix it |
| **Review** | Approve or reject |

<Note>
  Comment on the region at fault rather than describing the problem in general terms. A regeneration that knows *where* the problem is produces a targeted fix; one that only knows "make it better" rerolls the whole asset.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Regeneration replaces the asset in place. No version history, no rollback — download anything you might want back.
</Warning>

## A product must exist before it can be briefed

A product that is not in the catalogue cannot be briefed or generated. Adding one takes its page URL and builds the record, its facts, and a hero reference.
