## What takes how long

| Work | Lead time |
| --- | --- |
| **Statics** | Fastest. Generated or designed from existing assets |
| **Motion** | Medium. Built from stills and cutouts |
| **Video edit** | Medium. Re-cutting footage that exists |
| **Creator video** | Slowest. Casting, shipping, filming |

Under about five days to launch, statics or motion are your realistic options.

## Test timelines

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing judged for | 72 hours |
| Test window | About 6 days |
| Proving a winner | Roughly 15 orders |

<Warning>
  A client asking for results on day two is asking for noise. The engine withholds judgement while Meta is still learning. Reading early numbers as signal is how good ideas get killed.
</Warning>

## Quality standards

Check these before anything publishes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="One variable per idea">
    If an idea changes the angle and the audience and the format, the verdict tells you nothing you can act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Every claim carries evidence">
    Real numbers with spend attached.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claims are within brand rules">
    Approved claims verbatim, banned words absent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy matches the awareness level">
    If the opening line does not match the label, one of them is wrong.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Meta primary text has a hard limit of 400 characters. Aim for 125 or fewer.

## Approvals

| Gate | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Generation quote** | A price comes back. Nothing spends until confirmed |
| **Creative review** | Each asset approved or rejected |
| **Activation** | Paused ads switched on in Ads Manager |

Publishing never spends. That is a property of the system, so "it went live by accident" is not a failure mode here.

## Giving feedback

When rejecting an asset, mark the region at fault rather than describing the problem in general terms.

A regeneration that knows where the problem is produces a targeted fix. One that only knows "make it better" rerolls the whole asset, and you lose the parts that were working.

Score it too. Scores accumulate into the account's picture of what works.

Regeneration replaces an asset in place. There is no undo. Download anything you might want back.

## What gets recorded

Every action is attributed to a person, to Kenny or to the system, with the brand and the outcome. In a regulated category, that is the difference between a conversation and a problem.
