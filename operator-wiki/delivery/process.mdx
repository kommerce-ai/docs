Delivery is one loop repeated, not a project with an end. Research feeds briefs, briefs feed tests, tests feed learnings, and learnings feed the next round of research.

## The loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understand the brand">
    Positioning, products, unit economics. See [Positioning and economics](/operator-wiki/client-success/positioning-and-economics).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Research">
    Brand, competitors and customers — what to say and to whom. See [Research](/operator-wiki/delivery/research).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brief and produce">
    Angles become briefs; briefs become ads. See [Creative briefs](/operator-wiki/creative-testing/creative-briefs).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test">
    Publish paused, activate deliberately, read the daily calls. See [Publishing](/operator-wiki/performance/publishing-tests).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conclude and scale">
    Write the verdict, which writes the learning. Scale what won. See [Scaling](/operator-wiki/performance/scaling).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The gates

Three points where a person must act. They exist because each one is expensive to get wrong:

| Gate | What it protects |
| --- | --- |
| **Generation quote** | Credits. Generation returns a price first and spends nothing until confirmed |
| **Publish paused** | Spend. Nothing runs until someone activates it in Ads Manager |
| **Verdict** | Knowledge. A test only becomes a learning when a person writes it up |

<Warning>
  The third gate is the one that gets skipped, because nothing breaks when you skip it. Tests that run and never close leave the account no smarter than before you started.
</Warning>

## Where the work is written down

Repeatable procedures live in the [Kommerce library](https://github.com/kommerce-ai/kommerce-library) as skills and SOPs, organised by job. Before solving a recurring task from scratch, check whether someone has already written it down — see [Skills](/operator-wiki/resources/skills).
