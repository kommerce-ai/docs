Delivery is one loop repeated. Research feeds briefs, briefs feed tests, tests feed learnings, and learnings feed the next round of research.

## The loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understand the brand">
    Positioning, products, unit economics. See [Understanding the brand](/operator-wiki/client-success/the-brand).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Research">
    Brand, competitors and customers. What to say and to whom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brief and produce">
    Angles become briefs. Briefs become ads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test">
    Publish paused, activate deliberately, read the daily calls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conclude and scale">
    Write the verdict, which writes the learning. Scale what won.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The three gates

Each one exists because it is expensive to get wrong.

| Gate | What it protects |
| --- | --- |
| **Generation quote** | Credits. A price comes back first. Nothing spends until confirmed |
| **Publish paused** | Spend. Nothing runs until someone activates it in Ads Manager |
| **Verdict** | Knowledge. A test becomes a learning only when a person writes it up |

<Warning>
  The third gate is the one people skip, because nothing visibly breaks. Tests that run and never close leave the account no smarter than before you started.
</Warning>

## Where procedures live

Repeatable procedures live in the [Kommerce library](https://github.com/kommerce-ai/kommerce-library) as skills and SOPs, organised by job.

Before solving a recurring task from scratch, check whether someone already wrote it down. See [Skills](/operator-wiki/resources/skills).
