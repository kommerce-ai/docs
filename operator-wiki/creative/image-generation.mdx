## Step 1 — Brief

The brief collects context, then generates messaging.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Product** | Which product the creative sells. Required. |
| **Persona** | Who it speaks to, from the brand's personas. |
| **Awareness** | Which awareness level(s) to write for. |
| **Theme** | Optional steer — *"Summer refresh"*, *"science-backed trust"*, *"limited drop"*. |
| **Variations** | How many variations per awareness level. |

Generating produces an editable **angle, hooks, and CTA**. Review and edit before continuing — this is the entire point of the step existing.

When ready, click **Continue to templates**.

### Awareness is the highest-leverage field

It changes the first line of every ad. A problem-aware buyer needs the problem named; a product-aware buyer needs the mechanism; a brand-aware buyer needs a reason to act now. Writing one ad for all three lands with none of them.

Selecting all levels also multiplies your output — see the matrix below.

## Step 2 — Templates

Pick from the **Select Visual Blueprints** grid, grouped by product category.

Templates are the visual shell; the angles from your brief fill them. Before clicking Create, work out what you are about to generate:

```
angles × products × awareness levels × personas × variations
```

<Warning>
  This climbs fast. Three angles across three awareness levels, two personas, and three variations is 54 assets from a single click. Check the count before you Create — generation draws on the operator [credit balance](/operator-wiki/credits).
</Warning>

Start with a handful of templates. You can re-run the same brief against more once you have seen what a few produce.

## Step 3 — Generate

Kommerce renders and streams assets in as they finish, showing placeholders for work still in flight. You do not need to keep the tab focused.

Finished assets land in the brand's creative library.

## Getting more out of it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Narrow before you widen">
    One product, one persona, one awareness level, three variations. See whether the angle lands, then scale the matrix around what worked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Theme deliberately">
    It is the cheapest way to steer a whole batch without rewriting the brief. A campaign name, a seasonal hook, a claim you want led with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit hooks, not prompts">
    The hooks are the copy. Editing them directly is faster and more predictable than re-generating the brief hoping for different output.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits worth knowing

- **Regeneration overwrites in place.** No version history. Download anything you might want back.
- **Product imagery is the ceiling.** Templates composite your product photos; low-resolution or text-baked-in source images carry those problems into every asset.
- **Templates are grouped by product category.** If a brand's category has thin template coverage, that constrains the visual range more than the brief does.
