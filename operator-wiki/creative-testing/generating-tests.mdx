---
title: "Generating tests"
description: "Getting Kommerce to tell you what to test next, and turning that into a launchable test."
---

You do not start from a blank page. Kommerce reads the account and tells you what to test next, with the numbers behind it.

## Ask for recommendations

Each recommendation names the idea, what to do, and the evidence.

| Recommendation | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Iterate** | Something is working. Make another version of it |
| **New concepts** | Not enough different ideas in flight |
| **Fix the funnel** | The ads are fine. The problem is after the click |
| **Scale** | An idea has proven itself |
| **Hold** | Not enough evidence yet. Leave it alone |
| **Drop** | Stop spending on this |

Read the evidence before acting. A recommendation is a rule firing on data. You may know something it does not.

## Most new budget goes to ideas that already work

Kommerce puts about four out of five new slots into variations of ideas already passing or promising. One in five goes to new concepts.

The ratio is deliberate. The most common way testing goes wrong is abandoning something that was working to chase a new idea. You then get enough evidence on neither.

You can override this for a brand where it does not fit. Do it knowingly.

## Fix the funnel is the one people skip

When clicks are cheap but orders do not follow, more creative will not help.

<Warning>
  Generating more ads against a page that is not converting costs real money and moves nothing. It is the most expensive mistake in this product because it feels productive.
</Warning>

## Turning a recommendation into a test

A recommendation becomes a draft. Nothing is live yet. Check three things before launching.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The ideas">
    Each one gets its own ad set and budget. This is the real cost of the test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The budget">
    Each idea gets about two and a half times break-even CPA. Kommerce also holds back enough to confirm whatever passes, so it will refuse to launch an idea it could not afford to prove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The bar">
    Break-even CPA. Everything downstream is judged against it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

There are two gaps between a suggestion and money moving. A draft is not running, and publishing creates everything paused.

## Using ad sets that already run

You can point a test at live ad sets instead of launching new ones. Kommerce reads them as ideas and applies the same rules.

This is the fastest way to get value from an account you have just taken over. You get verdicts on what is already there without spending anything new.
