The brief says what the ad argues. Generation builds it.

## What the plan needs

Before generating, each ad needs a scene decided. Six things:

| Decision | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Shot type** | A close single-bottle shot, because the label is the identity |
| **Setting** | A bedside table in the morning |
| **Props** | Pale roses, white linen, an open book |
| **Light** | Soft daylight from the left |
| **Backdrop** | A pale blush wall, tone on tone with the label |
| **Copy zone** | The top 35%, a wall out of focus |

Plus placement: where the product sits, and how much of the frame it fills.

## Product truth overrides the brief

The model will get physical facts wrong unless the scene stops it.

- **A tiny product.** Frame waist-up or wider. Avoid tight hand shots and put a known object in frame. Models size a product to the hand holding it, so a 25 mm pendant comes out at 35 to 50 mm in a close hand shot whatever the words say.
- **The identity is on the label.** Use a front-facing close shot with the label readable.
- **Several variants.** Use a lineup from a real group photo. Generated lineups drift in shape.

## Meta's safe zones

Text outside these gets covered or cropped.

**4:5 (1080 × 1350).** Keep text out of the bottom 200 px. Meta's call to action covers it.

**9:16 (1080 × 1920).** The top 13% sits under the Reels bar. Below 65%, Meta's caption and button cover the image, so nothing important goes there. The right 120 px of the lower half sits under Meta's icons.

**Shooting both.** Render 9:16 and crop 4:5 from the middle 70%.

<Warning>
  Never put a button in the image. Meta adds its own, and two buttons read as a mistake.
</Warning>

## The 1-2-3 read

The eye should move through the ad in three beats. Each beat needs a reason to land and a reason to move on.

| Beat | Its job | What makes the eye land |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Product** | Stop the scroll | The brightest, sharpest, highest-contrast thing, with space around it |
| **2. Headline** | Say the idea | The biggest type, in the calmest area |
| **3. Offer or proof** | Give a reason to act | A smaller pocket with its own shape: a card, a badge, a price |

Beat 3 sits nearest Meta's button, so the read ends at the tap.

### Rhythm rules

1. Each beat is a clear step quieter than the last, roughly half the size or weight. Two equal elements fight and the eye stalls.
2. Gaps grow with rank. Headline to subhead is tight. Creative block to offer is a big gap. Closeness groups, distance separates.
3. One axis. Stack the beats on a shared centre line or left edge.
4. A simple path: a bounce, a Z or an L. At most one jump back, never a zigzag.
5. Three stops, no fourth. Props and highlights stay quieter than beat 3.

## Matched pairs share an image

For a matched pair, both versions use the same image and differ only in the variable's words. That is what makes the result attributable.

## Generation is quoted first

Asking to generate returns a price. Nothing is spent at that point. It runs only when you confirm.

Work out the count before confirming. It is roughly angles × products × awareness levels × personas × variations. Three angles across three awareness levels, two personas and three variations is 54 assets from one click.

<Warning>
  The wallet is a hard ceiling. A run that would exceed the balance is refused outright. It is never half-completed.
</Warning>

Renders take about two minutes.

## Reviewing what came back

Zoom in before judging. Text that looks wrong at thumbnail size is often fine close up.

Then score it, comment on the region at fault, regenerate, and approve or reject.

Comment on the region rather than describing the problem in general terms. A regeneration that knows where the problem is produces a targeted fix. One that only knows "make it better" rerolls the whole asset.

Regeneration replaces the asset in place. There is no undo.

## What goes wrong most

- Text in a safe zone, so Meta crops the headline
- A product scaled wrong because the shot was too tight
- Four stops instead of three, so the eye stalls
- Two elements at the same weight fighting for beat 1
- A generated lineup where the variants drift in shape
