---
title: "What is creative strategy?"
description: "The discipline between research and production — deciding what to say, to whom, and what you're trying to prove."
---

Creative strategy is the decision about **what to say and to whom**. Production is the decision about how it looks. Confusing the two produces beautiful ads that test nothing.

## What a strategist decides

<Steps>
  <Step title="Who it's for">
    Exactly one persona. Not a blend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What they already know">
    The awareness level, which decides what the opening line is allowed to say. See [Awareness levels](/operator-wiki/creative-testing/awareness-levels).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The angle">
    The claim or positioning being argued.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The one variable">
    What changes between versions, with everything else held constant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The mode">
    Static, video, motion or creator — chosen from evidence and timeline, not taste. See [UGC and creators](/operator-wiki/creative-testing/ugc-and-creators).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decide from evidence

Before any creative thinking, read what the account already knows: its **learnings**, its **patterns**, past tests, account numbers, and competitor research.

Each claim that reaches a brief carries a tag saying where it came from. How strong the evidence is gets stated plainly:

| Strength | Roughly |
| --- | --- |
| **Strong** | Repeated, with real spend behind it |
| **Likely** | Good signal, decent sample |
| **Leaning** | Suggestive |
| **Directional** | Under ~10 orders, or an early pattern |
| **Thin** | Barely anything |
| **Against** | Evidence points the other way |

<Warning>
  **Never contradict a learning silently.** If a test argues against something the account already established, cite it and say why re-testing is worth the money. Sometimes it is — but it should be a decision, not an oversight.
</Warning>

## Exploratory ideas are allowed

An idea with no supporting evidence can still be worth testing. Label it **exploratory** and keep it to the minimum budget.

What's not allowed is an unsupported idea dressed up as an evidenced one.

## Holding the variable constant

The entire value of a test is being able to attribute the result.

<Note>
  Format is held constant inside a test. If the strategy calls for two modes, that's two tests asking the same question — not one test with mixed formats.
</Note>
