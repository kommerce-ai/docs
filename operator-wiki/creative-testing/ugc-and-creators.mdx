Every test runs in exactly one mode. Format is held constant inside a test — if the strategy calls for two modes, that's two tests asking the same question.

## The four modes

| Mode | Who makes it | Lead time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Statics** | Designer, or generated | Fastest |
| **Video** | Editor, re-cutting existing footage | Medium |
| **Motion** | Motion designer, from stills and cutouts | Medium |
| **Trybe creators** | Creators sourced through Trybe | Slowest |

## Choosing one

In this order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="The format the evidence ran in">
    If the winning ad was a static, test the angle as statics first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brand rules">
    A brand that bans AI-generated people rules out whole modes before you start. Favour real photos, real footage, or creators.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Timeline">
    Under about five days to launch means statics, or motion and video from existing assets. Creators need casting, shipping and filming.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Budget">
    Video and creators cost more to make. Worth it when the test can fund a real read, or when the creative will be reused after the test.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  State the reason in the recommendation, not just the choice. "Statics — the current winner is logged as an image, and we're two days from launch" is a decision someone can disagree with. "Let's do statics" isn't.
</Tip>

## What creator briefs need

A creator brief is **direction, not a script**. You're briefing a person who will say it their own way.

| Part | What goes in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Matches the persona |
| **Talking points** | Customer phrases, in her own words |
| **Must say** | Approved claims, verbatim |
| **Never say** | The brand's banned words |

<Note>
  For matched pairs, have one creator film both versions in a single session. That holds the creator constant, so the difference you measure is the variable — not one person being more watchable than another.
</Note>

## Video and motion notes

**Video.** When re-cutting a proven ad, leave the body alone and change only the variable — usually the hook or the end card. Never write voiceover for footage you haven't seen.

**Motion.** The hook frame has to read with sound off inside one second. Five words a frame at most, one movement idea per frame. Describe the motion an editor would build.

<Warning>
  Most feed views start muted. A video whose first second only works with sound is a video most people never see.
</Warning>
