You do not need to be a Meta expert to run Kommerce. A few structural facts decide whether your tests mean anything.

## The hierarchy

| Level | Holds | Decides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Campaign** | Ad sets | Objective, and budget if shared |
| **Ad set** | Ads | Audience, placements, and budget if per-ad-set |
| **Ad** | Creative and copy | What people see |

In Kommerce, one idea equals one ad set. Versions of that idea are ads inside it, sharing its budget. That is why ideas cost money and versions do not.

## Budget: shared or per ad set

This is the most consequential setting in a test.

**Per ad set** gives each idea its own money, so each gets a fair read. Default to this for testing.

**Shared across the campaign** lets Meta move budget to whatever looks best early.

<Warning>
  A shared budget will starve one side of a test before it produces a verdict. Meta optimises for immediate return. You are buying information. Those goals conflict, and Meta wins.
</Warning>

Shared budgets are a scaling tool, not a testing tool.

## The learning phase

Meta needs conversions before delivery stabilises. Until then performance swings for reasons unrelated to your creative.

This is why nothing is judged for 72 hours.

## Laying out a test

Each idea gets its own ad set and budget. Across the ad sets in a test, keep these identical:

- Audience and exclusions
- Placements
- Bid strategy
- Campaign objective

A test where one idea runs on different placements is not a test of the idea. You will attribute a placement effect to your creative and carry that wrong conclusion forward.

## Naming

Copy the pattern the brand's live ads already use. Change only the test number, awareness code, slug and version.

Consistent naming is what lets patterns group ads by persona, awareness and angle later. Inconsistent naming destroys that quietly, and you will not notice until you want the analysis.

## Campaigns

Kommerce cannot create campaigns. A new campaign is built in Ads Manager or in Kommerce's publish flow. Everything else in a test can be created for you.

## Why tracking decides everything

Meta optimises toward the conversions it can see. Bad signal produces bad optimisation and bad reporting at once, in the same direction, invisibly.

When purchase tracking is unreliable, Kommerce stops drawing conclusions from the checkout and purchase steps. Those measure tracking, not ads.
