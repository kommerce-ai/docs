Media Buying covers the path from approved creative to entities in Meta Ads Manager. Open **Media Buying** in the sidebar for the brand, or go to `/campaign/create`.

<Info>
  **Publishing always creates campaigns, ad sets, and ads in a PAUSED state.** Nothing spends until someone activates it in Ads Manager. This is deliberate — publish is not launch.
</Info>

## The three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select or generate creatives">
    Pick assets from the brand's library, or generate new ones inline. This is the same creative pipeline described in [Creative production](/operator-wiki/creative/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ad set and ad review">
    Set the budget mode, targeting, and copy, then approve each ad. This is the step that decides how the campaign learns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to Meta">
    Kommerce builds the launch payload and creates the entities. Everything arrives paused.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2 in detail

This is where the real decisions happen.

**Budget mode** — choose CBO (campaign-level budget) or ABO (ad-set-level budgets). The choice interacts with the Structure dimension of the [Meta audit](/operator-wiki/meta-audit): fragmenting a modest budget across many ad sets is the most common structural failure it flags.

**Targeting** — age, location, and audience configuration per ad set.

**Optimisation and pixel** — the conversion event and the pixel the ad set optimises toward.

**Copy and CTA URL** — the ad text and destination.

Each ad is approved individually. Unapproved ads do not publish.

## Managing a live campaign

Once published, open the campaign from the campaigns list to manage it. Operator users can also work across accounts from **Meta Ads** at `/meta-ads`.

## Linking the Meta account

A brand needs a Meta ad account linked before anything can publish. The brand's home surface shows the linked account and lets you change it.

The brand grants **partner access** to your Business Manager directly in Meta, then enters its own ad account ID and clicks **Verify**. Kommerce checks server-side whether that account now appears in your accessible client accounts.

<Note>
  The brand only ever submits its own ad account ID. Your list of other clients' accounts is never exposed to them — the lookup happens server-side.
</Note>

An ad account can be linked to **at most one brand**. If verification rejects an ID as already linked, that is the anti-hijack guard doing its job, not a bug.

## When publishing fails

Most publish failures trace to one cause, and it is not the one the error message suggests.

A publish touches two different Meta assets with two different permission requirements:

| What is created | Acts on | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign, ad set, image upload | The **ad account** | A role on the ad account |
| **Ad creative** | The **Page** — it publishes *as* the Page | A task on the Page (Advertise or Create Content) |

A credential can have full ad-account access and **no Page access**. When that happens, the campaign, ad set, and image upload all succeed — then the ad creative fails with a generic error that says nothing about Pages.

<Warning>
  If a publish fails partway through with an unhelpful "Invalid parameter" error, check Page permissions first. "Same Meta account" does not mean "same access" — two connections for the same business can differ on which Pages they may act as.
</Warning>

If a publish fails after the campaign has been created, Kommerce rolls back and deletes the campaign, so you will not be left with a half-built structure.

## Acting on audit findings

The [Meta audit](/operator-wiki/meta-audit) is read-only and will never change the account for you. Its actions map to surfaces here:

| Finding | Where you fix it |
| --- | --- |
| Fragmented structure | Budget mode and ad set setup in Step 2 |
| Stale creative | [Creative production](/operator-wiki/creative/overview), then a new campaign |
| Spend below the kill line | Ads Manager — pause the offenders |
| Broken pixel | Meta Events Manager, outside Kommerce |
