## Setting up communication

Do this in the first week. Communication invented per-request is where delivery quality leaks.

Each brand gets a member-only Slack channel, a ClickUp space and a Drive folder when you add it. The channel is the brand's thread: questions, approvals, and the recurring report.

<Warning>
  Slack returns nothing for a channel the bot is not in, and the report degrades to a quiet note rather than an error. A silent daily pulse is almost always this. Invite the bot, then re-verify.
</Warning>

## The daily pulse

Kommerce writes a recurring account report from Meta performance, Slack discussion and ClickUp tasks. It scores the account Green, Yellow or Red across four areas.

| Area | What it watches |
| --- | --- |
| **Activity** | Is work actually happening |
| **Efficiency** | Is spend producing orders |
| **Creative** | Is the creative pool fresh and varied |
| **Coverage** | Are the bases covered |

It also summarises what the team is working on, sets an agenda and ranks suggestions.

Read it before the client does.

## Agreeing approvals

Settle three things up front.

| What | Who decides |
| --- | --- |
| **Creative** | Who approves ads before they publish |
| **Spend** | Who may raise budget, and by how much, without asking |
| **Claims** | Who signs off anything factual |

Claims sign-off is not optional in supplements or skincare.

Every action is attributed to a person, to Kenny or to the system, so "who approved this" is answerable later.

## Kickoff

Kickoff happens once the brand is launch ready.

Before the call, run the account audit, check the still-needed list, and know whether the bar is a real figure or an assumed one.

### What to set expectations on

**Testing produces verdicts, not instant wins.** Nothing is judged in the first 72 hours. A client expecting day-two results will read a working test as a failing one.

**Most ideas lose.** The job is finding the few that win cheaply. Say this before the first losing test.

**Publishing is not spending.** Everything arrives paused. Clients find this reassuring when it is explained up front and alarming when they discover it mid-launch.

Do not promise a launch date off "onboarding complete". That means the steps are done. It does not mean Meta will let the account spend.

## The weekly rhythm

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the daily pulse">
    Anything Red gets dealt with before anything else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the day's test calls">
    Approve what makes sense. Overrule what you know better.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep ideas in flight">
    An account with nothing testing is an account learning nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close what's finished">
    A concluded test writes a learning. An open one writes nothing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to watch for

| Signal | What it usually means |
| --- | --- |
| Pulse Red on Creative | The pool has gone stale |
| Pulse Red on Efficiency | Spend is not producing orders. Check the audit before the creative |
| No tests closing | Verdicts are reached and not written up |
| Every test exploratory | Nobody is reading patterns and learnings before briefing |

An account where tests run but never close has no memory. You will re-test answered questions and pay for the answer twice.
