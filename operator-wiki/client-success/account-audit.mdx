---
title: "Auditing a Meta account"
description: "The read-only diagnostic that tells you whether an account is safe to spend against."
---

Run this before the first campaign on any account with existing history. It never changes the account.

The most common finding is broken tracking. Every optimisation made on top of that is wasted work, so this comes first.

## The four dimensions

They are graded in a fixed order, because problems upstream invalidate conclusions downstream.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Signal — is the tracking trustworthy?">
    Pixel freshness and event quality. If Signal is Red, every number below it is suspect and the audit says so.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Structure — is budget consolidated enough to learn?">
    Fragmentation across ad sets, budget mode, and whether the account shape supports scale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Waste — where is spend going that shouldn't?">
    Spend below the kill line past the learning window, zombie campaigns, abandoned winners.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Creative — is the pool fresh and diverse?">
    Ad age, share of spend on stale creative, frequency, hook rate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reading a grade

Each dimension gets **Green**, **Yellow**, **Red** or **Blind**, plus a one-line receipt.

A receipt is a number, not a characterisation:

> Waste — RED — $18,400 of last-90d spend sat below 1.3 ROAS past day 3, across 4 campaigns.

**Blind** means the data for that dimension came back degraded. A verdict from partial data is legitimate. The audit tells you what it could not see rather than guessing.

<Warning>
  Signal Red is the one that matters most. It means the numbers underneath are unreliable, so fixing anything else first is guesswork. Deal with tracking before you touch structure, waste or creative.
</Warning>

## The ranked actions

The audit returns the top actions in priority order. It will not make them for you.

| Finding | Where it gets fixed |
| --- | --- |
| Tracking problems | Meta and the site. Outside Kommerce |
| Structural problems | Campaign setup in Ads Manager |
| Waste | Pausing in Ads Manager |
| Stale creative | A new test, then new campaigns |

## When to run it

- Before the first campaign on an inherited account
- Before kickoff, so you can bring findings to the call
- When performance drops for no obvious reason
- Before a client asks whether the account is ready to scale

## Account type

An account with little history produces a thin audit. That is correct behaviour rather than a fault. There is less to diagnose, and the audit says so instead of inventing findings.
