Onboarding produces the brand context everything else reads from. Angle research, creative briefs and reporting all degrade to generic output when it is incomplete.

## Adding the brand

Open **Brands** at `/agency/brands` and click **Add a brand**.

Enter the name and email, and the website if you have it. The website is worth supplying. It is what the AI prefill reads.

You can **Add brand only** to create the record without starting, or **Start Onboarding** to begin.

Creating a brand also provisions its workspace: a member-only Slack channel, a ClickUp space and a Drive folder.

## Choosing who does the work

A preset sets the default assignee for all three steps. You are choosing a starting point, not a permanent arrangement.

| Preset | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Let the brand do it** | Every step assigned to the brand. An invite goes out |
| **You do it for the brand** | Every step assigned to your team |

The most reliable split in practice is you taking Brand details and Products & assets, and the brand taking Connect Meta. The first two are faster from the website scrape. The third has to happen inside their own Business Manager.

Assignment can change on any step that is not yet complete. A completed step is locked.

## The invite

The brand gets an invite linking to a signup URL with a one-time code. Signing up through that link binds their account to you.

<Warning>
  A brand that signs up without the code lands as a self-serve brand under the house agency, not under you. There is no clean way to re-parent it. Always send the invite from Kommerce rather than telling a client to "just sign up".
</Warning>

## The three steps

| Step | Produces | Can be AI-drafted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Brand details** | Identity, voice, ICP, competitors, budget | Yes, from the website |
| **Products & assets** | Products, logo, photos, website | Yes |
| **Connect Meta ad account** | Partner access to your Business Manager | No |

A step moves from not started, to drafted if Kommerce prefilled it, to in progress, to done. Connect Meta works differently. It goes from not started, to submitted, to done, because Kommerce verifies it with Meta rather than taking someone's word for it.

Any unfinished step can be marked blocked.

### What the labels mean

| Label | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **This step needs you** | Assigned to you, not finished |
| **Drafted — review** | AI prefilled it. Someone needs to check it |
| **In progress** | Started, not finished |
| **Waiting on brand** / **Waiting on agency** | Assigned to the other party |
| **Done** | Complete |

The chip reads "Waiting on agency", not "operator". The product has not been renamed.

## Connecting Meta

This is the step that stalls most often. It has no draft, it happens inside the brand's Business Manager, and it only completes when the server verifies it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The brand copies your agency ID">
    Kommerce shows your Business Manager ID with a copy button. The control is labelled **Copy agency ID**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They grant partner access in Meta">
    Scoped to the ad account, page and pixel. They stay the owner and can revoke it any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They enter their own ad account ID">
    In the form `act_` followed by the number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kommerce verifies">
    A live call using your credentials checks whether that account now appears in your accessible client accounts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The brand only ever submits their own ad account ID. Your list of other clients' accounts is never shown to them.

### When it fails

Work through these in order. The first is by far the most common.

| Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Access granted to the wrong Business Manager | Re-check the agency ID they pasted against the one Kommerce shows |
| Not yet propagated | Wait and retry |
| Wrong ID type | It must be the ad account, `act_` prefixed |
| Partner added but assets not assigned | Adding the partner is not enough on its own |
| Already linked to another brand | Find the other brand. Do not unlink blindly |

An ad account can be linked to one brand only. A successful lookup proves the ID is in your shared list. It does not prove this brand owns it.

## Two finish lines

**Intake complete** means Brand details and Products & assets are done. That is two steps, not three.

**Launch ready** means intake plus Connect Meta. This unlocks **Start kickoff** and is what marks the brand onboarded.

## What still blocks spending

The readiness list is separate from the launch-ready status. These items are required, and every one is owned by the brand.

**Required:** logo, product photos, website access, Meta partner access, business verification, Meta ad account, payment method.

**Optional:** product video, Instagram, Meta Pixel.

Each item moves from missing, to received, to verified. Only verified counts.

<Warning>
  Launch ready can be true while business verification is still pending, because that item does not feed the status. Meta enforces it regardless. Check the still-needed list separately before promising a launch date.
</Warning>

Business verification and payment method are the two with real external latency. Meta can take days. Start business verification before anything else.

Treat the Meta Pixel as required in practice. Without it firing correctly, performance cannot be measured, and every decision made on top of bad tracking is unreliable.
