Onboarding produces the brand context everything else reads from. Angle research, creative briefs, and reporting all degrade to generic output when it is incomplete, so it is worth understanding the model rather than clicking through it.

## Three steps

| Step | Produces | Can be AI-drafted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Brand details** | Identity, voice, ICP, competitors, budget | Yes — from the website |
| **Products & assets** | Products, logo, photos, video, website | Yes — scrape plus uploads |
| **Connect Meta ad account** | Partner access to your Business Manager | No — human only |

The first two can be prefilled by scraping the brand's site. The third never is: it requires a human to act inside Meta, so it has no draft state.

## Step status

Each step moves through its own state machine:

A step moves from **not started**, to **drafted** if Kommerce prefilled it, to **in progress**, to **done**.

A step can jump straight from drafted to done if whoever owns it accepts the draft as-is. **Connect Meta** works differently: it goes from not started, to submitted, to done — because Kommerce has to verify it with Meta rather than take someone's word for it.

Any unfinished step can be marked **blocked** when it's waiting on the other side or on something external.

What you see in the UI is derived from status and assignee:

| Label | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **This step needs you** | Assigned to you, not finished |
| **Drafted — review** | AI prefilled it; someone needs to check it |
| **In progress** | Started, not finished |
| **Waiting on brand** / **Waiting on agency** | Assigned to the other party |
| **Done** | Complete |

<Note>
  The chip reads **Waiting on agency**, not "operator" — the product has not been renamed, so the label still uses the old word.
</Note>

## Who does the work

Every step is assigned either to the brand or to your team, set from a preset when you add the brand and changeable per step afterwards.

<Note>
  Assignment can be changed on any step that is **not yet complete**. A completed step is locked; the server rejects reassignment rather than silently reopening it. Reassign before it finishes, or reopen the step first.
</Note>

This is what makes mixed splits work — "we'll handle Meta access, the brand fills in their own details" is a supported configuration, not a workaround.

## Two finish lines

This is the part people conflate, and it matters because one of them is the go-live decision.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Intake complete" icon="list-check">
    **Brand details** and **Products & assets** are done — the two steps that put brand information in. Connect Meta is not part of it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Launch ready" icon="rocket">
    Intake done **and** Connect Meta complete. This is the go-live gate: it unlocks **Start kickoff** and is what sets the brand to onboarded.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

So intake is two steps, not three. The gap between the two milestones is exactly one thing: Meta access verified.

<Note>
  The readiness ledger does **not** gate launch readiness. Required items that are still missing appear as the **still needed** list, which is a chase list rather than a lock. See [Launch readiness](/operator-wiki/client-success/launch-readiness).
</Note>

## Progress

The progress bar counts **steps only** — completed steps out of all three, so it is not the same denominator as the intake milestone (which counts two).

It deliberately does not blend in readiness items. Connect Meta alone produces four of them, so a blended bar would jump 40% in one action and move non-linearly. Granularity lives in the step count and the "still needed" list instead.
