This is the path from never having opened Kommerce to running a client account on your own.

Each module ends in something you do, not something you read. The reading is short on purpose. The [Reference](/operator-wiki/client-success/onboarding) tab holds the full detail, and the lessons link into it where you need it.

<CourseMap />

## How to use it

**Work in order.** You cannot brief a test until you can read an account, and you cannot scale until you can read a verdict. The sequence is the lesson.

**Do the checkpoints on the demo brand.** Never on a live client account. A checkpoint is the only part anybody else sees, and it is what says you are ready for the next module.

**Progress is yours alone.** The ticks live in this browser. They clear if you clear site data, and nobody can see them. Submitting a checkpoint is what puts something on the record.
