The brief states what each idea is testing, so that when the verdict arrives you know what was proven. Write it badly and a weak creative makes the test read as a loss for the idea, when it was really a loss for the execution.

Before writing a word, read what the account already knows: the brand, the learnings, the patterns, past tests, the account numbers, and what competitors keep running.

Every claim that reaches the brief carries a tag saying where it came from. A line with no tag does not belong in the hypothesis or the evidence.

## The hypothesis

One sentence, under 600 characters, in a fixed shape.

<HypothesisBuilder />

Most people write the first four parts and stop. The last clause is the one that makes the test honest, because it forces you to say what would argue against you before the money goes out. If you cannot find any counter-evidence, you have not looked.

## Evidence rows

Evidence is a fact with a number and a spend figure attached, tagged with where it came from, and weighted by how much it can carry.

<EvidenceChecker />

Strength runs Strong, Likely, Leaning, Directional, Thin, Against. Under about ten orders, nothing is better than directional, however good the number looks.

## Levers

A lever is how a version argues its angle: Offer, Logic, Social, Emotion or Novelty. Record it on every creative.

<LeverPicker />

Each hook inside a version should pull a different lever too, unless the lever itself is what you are testing. Ads in one ad set should not be paraphrases of each other.

## The creative row

Each ad gets a row carrying the Meta headline with its character count, the button, the composition, the on-image text, the lever, a real reference, and the primary text.

Describe a composition a designer could execute without guessing. Describe the idea, not render settings.

<Warning>
  Never invent an ad reference. Link to a real ad in the Meta Ad Library or your saved research, or
  leave the field empty.
</Warning>

## Check yourself

<Check
  question="Which of these is an evidence row?"
  options={[
    "Offer-led statics convert well for this brand",
