## What a good submission looks like

Your reviewer is checking four things, in this order.

**The hypothesis could be wrong.** It names a change, a result, something to compare against, a reason, and the strongest evidence against it. A hypothesis that cannot fail is not a hypothesis.

**The evidence carries its own weight.** Every row has a tag, a number and a spend figure. Nothing thin is labelled strong.

**The test is readable.** One variable. Both versions built to win. Volume matched. What each version must not do is written down.

**You stopped where you were meant to stop.** The test is a draft. Nothing is live. Generating creative and publishing are separate, deliberate steps, and a checkpoint that published something has failed regardless of how good the brief was.
