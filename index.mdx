Kommerce runs a performance marketing department as one system: it onboards the brand, audits the account, generates and tests creative, and scales what wins.

Every account is paired with a human operator. The system does the volume; the operator decides what goes live.

## How the work runs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Onboard the brand">
    Brand details, products and assets, and access to the Meta ad account. This is the context everything downstream reads from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Audit and research">
    Check the account is trustworthy before spending against it, and find the angles worth testing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test creative">
    Turn angles into briefs, briefs into ads, and ads into tests that run against a break-even bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scale the winners">
    An idea that proves itself twice gets real budget behind it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Kommerce is a managed service. The department is installed into your business and run alongside your team — it isn't a tool you're left to operate on your own.
</Info>

## How you stay in control

- **Nothing spends without approval.** Ads publish **paused**; a person activates them.
- **Generation is quoted first.** You see the cost before credits move, never after.
- **Every action is attributed.** Who did what, for which brand, and what happened.

## Where it plugs in

Kommerce works with the stack you already run — Shopify, Meta, Google, TikTok, Slack and ClickUp. Operators can also drive it from Claude through [the MCP connection](/operator-wiki/resources/mcp).

## Verticals

Tailored versions of the department run for supplements and nutraceuticals, food and beverage, skincare and beauty, home services, and ecommerce and DTC.
