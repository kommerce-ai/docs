Every account is paired with a human operator. AI does the volume; the operator reviews the work before anything launches.

<Info>
  Kommerce is a managed service, not a self-serve tool. There is no package to install and no API to call — the department is installed into your business and run alongside your team.
</Info>

## How the engagement runs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Week one — the department is installed">
    The system launches and starts executing all five jobs against your store. First deliverables land within days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Months one to six — the brand is built">
    Weekly refinement of angles, creative, spend, and metrics. You keep decision-making authority throughout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ongoing — you own the operation">
    You are left with an operating brand and the internal understanding to run it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you stay in control

- **Nothing goes live without approval.** Every ad and every spend decision is approved before launch.
- **Weekly working sessions.** A standing session to review performance and set the next week's direction.
- **Friday reporting.** A weekly readout on CAC and the strategy behind the numbers.

## Where it plugs in

Kommerce works with the stack you already run — Shopify, Meta, Google, TikTok, Slack, and ClickUp.

## Verticals

Kommerce runs tailored versions of the department for supplements and nutraceuticals, food and beverage, skincare and beauty, home services, and ecommerce and DTC.
