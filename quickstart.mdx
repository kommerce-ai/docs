Getting started with Kommerce means scoping the engagement, then installing the department. There is nothing to install on your side — the first week is setup and first output, run by us with your approval.

## Start the conversation

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Book a call" icon="phone" href="https://usekommerce.com/book">
    Walk through your brand and scope the engagement. You will be told directly if you are not a fit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Send your store URL" icon="link" href="https://usekommerce.com">
    Skip the call for now and see what the first week of work would look like against your store.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Pricing depends on scope and is set on the call rather than published. There are two ways to engage: **build with the department** if you are an operator scaling a brand, or **install into your business** if you are an established brand with existing traffic and a team to keep.
</Note>

## What to have ready

Onboarding is faster when access to your existing stack is ready to grant. Kommerce works with:

| Area | Tools |
| --- | --- |
| Store | Shopify |
| Ad platforms | Meta, Google, TikTok |
| Communication | Slack |
| Project tracking | ClickUp |

You do not need all of these. Bring what you already run — the department installs into your existing infrastructure rather than replacing it.

## Your first week

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scope">
    A call to understand the brand, the offer, the current numbers, and whether Kommerce is the right fit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access">
    Grant access to your store, ad accounts, and communication channels so the department can work against real data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install">
    All five jobs start running against your store — angle research, copy, creative, media buying, and reporting. First deliverables land within days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Your operator brings the work to you. Nothing goes live until you approve the ads and the spend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="First Friday report">
    A readout on CAC and the strategy behind it, then the direction for week two.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The weekly rhythm

After week one, the engagement settles into a repeating cycle:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Working session">
    Review last week's performance with your operator and set direction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Production">
    New angles, copy, and creative are produced and queued for approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval and launch">
    You approve; spend goes live behind what is working.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Friday report">
    CAC and strategy readout closes the week.
  </Step>
</Steps>
